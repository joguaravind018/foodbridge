Food safety & pickup readiness

A reminder checklist for coordinators. This is not a food-safety certification.

✓
🕒
Check preparation time. Confirm food is still within the safe handling window before accepting a listing.
🌡️
Confirm storage. Verify whether food was refrigerated, frozen, kept warm, or stored at room temperature.
⚠️
Review urgency and condition. Prioritize near-deadline pickups; reject food that is spoiled or has uncertain handling.
📦
Handover checklist. Confirm quantity, packaging, pickup contact, and any known allergen information before dispatch.

How matching works

Transparent rule-based scoring — no AI claim.

◎
📍
Distance: closer eligible NGOs are preferred.
🍱
Food type: NGOs that list the offered food in their needs receive a higher score.
📦
Capacity: sufficient available capacity improves the recommendation.
⏱️
Urgency: donations with short pickup deadlines receive a priority boost.

Prototype notice: matching is rule-based (distance, quantity fit, capacity, urgency), not AI. Impact counts only donations confirmed Completed. All figures and locations are sample data for demonstration; no measured results or real NGO partnerships are claimed. Map tiles require an internet connection.
